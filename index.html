Happy Birthday, My Raakshasii ❤️

Happy Birthday to my favorite, most dangerously adorable troublemaker in this entire universe! Today is the day the cosmos decided I needed someone to completely wreck my composure, steal my heart, and become my absolute favorite person to ever exist.

I know how deeply your heart reveres Sri Radha & Krishna—how your eyes light up with pure tenderness whenever their names are spoken. Seeing that devotion in you makes me love you a thousand times more. Today, I join my hands and pray to the Divine Couple of Vrindavan: may Radha Rani shower you with boundless love and endless laughter, and may Lord Krishna forever protect your sweet smile and crown your life with eternal peace.

You don't need to be quiet or serious to be perfect in my eyes. You just have to be my raakshasii—my sweet menace, my best friend, my sweetest addiction, and the one whose laughter makes my entire world weightless.

"May Radha & Krishna bless you with eternity... while I spend every second loving you."
